      Common PG problems, and how we fix them

      Tap a problem to see the fix.

      Safety worries, especially late at night

      Fix: CCTV on entrances and corridors, a locked main gate after a set hour, a visitor register, and a warden or caretaker on call. Share the emergency numbers on every floor.

      Mess food that does not suit you

      Fix: A self-cooking kitchen, so you cook what you like, when you like, and save money. Keep it neat with labelled shelves, fridge space and a simple cleaning rota.

      Water shortage and power cuts

      Fix: Overhead tanks plus a sump with a timer-based pump schedule, inverter or generator backup for lights, fans and Wi-Fi, and regular tank cleaning every quarter.

      Slow Wi-Fi for work and study

      Fix: Fibre broadband with one access point per floor, a backup connection, and a simple rule against heavy downloads at peak hours.

      Cleanliness and pests

      Fix: A daily cleaning checklist signed by staff, weekly deep cleaning of bathrooms, monthly pest control, and sealed dustbins on each floor.

      Slow repairs and unclear rules

      Fix: One WhatsApp number for complaints with a 48-hour fix promise, a written rule sheet at check-in, and clear notice, deposit and rent terms in the agreement.
